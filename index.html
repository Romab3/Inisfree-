About Roma

"You are finite. And because of that, what you choose to do matters."— Oliver Burkeman

This is the quiet truth most people avoid. You don't need to do more. You need to choose more carefully.

I'm Roma Bisnath. Leadership coach, HR strategist, facilitator, and yoga teacher. I've spent nearly two decades working with leaders and organisations across the world.

I'm also an immigrant, a mum, and someone who has lived through grief and loss. I've learned that you have to create your own sense of safety within yourself, so you can live intentionally. Because showing up is hard. And showing up in alignment with who you actually want to be, that takes uncommon courage.

Through all of it, I found myself returning to the same question, again and again: who am I, beneath all of this?

My work is grounded in years of practice, a deep foundation in leadership and psychology, and a belief that the body often knows things the mind hasn't caught up with yet.

I work with women who are capable and driven but quietly exhausted. Women who want to live on their own terms, with more clarity and intention.

The origin

The Inisfree Collective was inspired by a poem I studied years ago. The Lake Isle of Innisfree.

It speaks of stillness, of peace that comes slowly, of a return to self.

Years later, as I began thinking about how to help people reconnect with who they are beneath the noise, the poem came back to me.

And I realised. This is what I am here to create. A space to soften. To realign. To remember.
