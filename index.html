About Roma

You built a life
through effort.
Then life asked something
deeper of you.

I'm Roma Bisnath. Leadership coach, HR strategist, facilitator, and yoga teacher. I've spent nearly two decades working with leaders and organisations across the world.

I'm also an immigrant who knows that security is never guaranteed. Someone who has moved through grief and loss while still showing up. Someone who has learned, again and again, that the question underneath all the doing is: who am I, beneath all of this?

That question is at the heart of everything I do. My work is grounded in nearly two decades of practice, a deep foundation in leadership and human psychology, and a belief that the body knows things the mind hasn't caught up with yet.

I work with women who are capable and driven and quietly exhausted. Who want to keep going — but differently. With more clarity, more ease, and more of themselves intact.

The origin

The Inisfree Collective was inspired by a poem I studied years ago. The Lake Isle of Innisfree.

It speaks of stillness, of peace that comes slowly, of a return to self.

Years later, as I began thinking about how to help people reconnect with who they are beneath the noise, the poem came back to me.

And I realised. This is what I am here to create. A space to soften. To realign. To remember.
