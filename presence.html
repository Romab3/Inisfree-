What your system may be telling you

You’ve gone quiet inside. Sometimes it’s the only way to survive a season. But you’ve noticed you’ve drifted from something, and you’re not yet sure what to do.

What the science tells us

Polyvagal theory describes a dorsal vagal state: the body’s shutdown response when fight-or-flight has gone on too long. It looks like numbness, flatness, “I’m fine.” It’s a protective mechanism. Gentle, body-based practices are some of the most evidence-supported ways back into the window of tolerance.

What you may need most

A slow way back into your body and your own preferences. Practices that reconnect you to sensation, choice, and self.
